How this was counted

Only messages a person typed (including accepted suggestions and queued messages) in ~/.claude/projects are counted. Subagent traffic, system notices and the summary inserted after /compact are excluded. Active time is the number of 5-minute bins with any recorded activity between a prompt and the next one, so time away is excluded and long autonomous Claude runs are included. Task types and repeat keys are labels Claude assigned after reading session titles and messages. Friction and outcomes are the per-session analysis /insights left in ~/.claude/usage-data/facets. Quotes and session titles are shown as written.
